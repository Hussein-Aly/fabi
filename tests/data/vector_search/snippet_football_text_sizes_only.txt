## Ball Sizes.docx

        ### Football (soccer) sizes

        Size 1: “Mini” ball, about 45–50 cm circumference; fun skills ball for all ages, especially toddlers.

        Size 2: About 55–56 cm circumference; for kids around 3–5 years and technique practice.

        Size 3: About 58–60 cm circumference; typically used for kids up to around 8–9 years.

        Size 4: About 63–66 cm circumference; usually for children roughly 9–12 years.

        Size 5: Full-size adult match ball, 68–70 cm circumference; used from about 12–13 years up to adults in official games.
